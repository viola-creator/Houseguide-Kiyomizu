Your stay

How things work

Breakfast
Breakfast is served at 8:00 AM (closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays). If you'd prefer a different time, please give us a heads-up. Otherwise, we're happy to reserve a table for you — you can settle the bill at the end of your meal.
Air Conditioning
Each room has its own panel on the wall with a remote beside it. Press power, then select cooling (冷房) or heating (暖房). Adjust the temperature with the arrows.
Cleaning
For stays of 2 nights or more, housekeeping visits every 3 days between 11 AM – 1 PM. Let us know if you'd like to skip or need extra privacy.
Quiet hours
After midnight. Sound carries easily through traditional wooden architecture. Please be mindful of our neighbors, especially in the evenings.
House Rules
Smoking is strictly prohibited on the premises. Please be mindful of the noise level out of respect for our neighbors. This is a residential neighborhood — we kindly ask that you treat it like home.

Before you leave

Checkout

  • Please check out by 11:00 AM so we can prepare the house for the next guest.
  • Leave used towels in the bathroom. No need to strip the beds.
  • Turn off all air conditioning and heating units before you go.
  • Close all windows and make sure the front door locks behind you.
  • Let us know ahead of time if you need help with luggage — we can arrange delivery to your next destination.
